# Privacy Policy — Spinly

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

Spinly collects no personal data, tracks no user activity, and stores all user data locally on the device.

## What this means

- **No accounts.** Spinly does not ask you to sign up, log in, or provide an email address.
- **No data collection.** We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.
- **No tracking.** Spinly contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
- **Local storage only.** The wheels you create, your spin history, and your preferences (theme, language, wheel colors) are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this data.
- **No third parties.** Spinly does not send your data to any third-party service.

## Device features

- **Language:** Spinly reads your device language once to choose between Turkish and English. This is not transmitted anywhere.
- **Sound and vibration:** Used only for spin feedback. Spinly does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or files.

## Children

Spinly does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new date.
